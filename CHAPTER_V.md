# CHAPTER V
## CONCLUSION AND RECOMMENDATIONS

### 5.1 Conclusion
Based on the findings of the study, **EventFlow: A Centralized Event Evaluation Management System with Decision Support and Sentiment Analysis** effectively addresses the fragmented and inefficient event evaluation practices at Caraga State University – Cabadbaran City Campus (CSUCC). The system successfully integrates the entire event evaluation lifecycle—from event creation and document-based approval to participant management, payment tracking, and AI-driven data analysis—into a unified platform.

The system demonstrated high performance and reliability across all evaluated domains. According to the ISO/IEC 25010 evaluation, EventFlow achieved a grand mean of **4.51 (Very Good)**, with particularly high ratings in Performance Efficiency and Usability (**4.72**). Furthermore, the sentiment analysis module, powered by the **XLM-RoBERTa** model with LoRA fine-tuning, achieved an impressive **90.34% accuracy** in classifying multilingual feedback (English, Tagalog, and Bisaya).

The study confirms that the implementation of EventFlow significantly reduces manual administrative workload, improves feedback accuracy, and supports evidence-based decision-making. By transforming raw textual feedback into actionable insights through its Rule-Based Reasoning (RBR) engine and NLP service, the system provides student organizations and university administrators with a transparent and practical solution for continuous quality improvement in campus events.

### 5.2 Recommendation
Based on the successful development and positive evaluation of **EventFlow**, the researchers recommend its full implementation within the student organizations and administrative offices of Caraga State University – Cabadbaran City Campus. To ensure the system remains effective and evolves with the needs of the university, the following recommendations are proposed for future development:

- **Integration of Optical Character Recognition (OCR)**: Future developers should consider integrating OCR technology to allow for the automated processing of printed evaluation codes or scanned physical forms, further reducing manual data entry requirements.
- **Enhanced Mobile Accessibility**: While the system is currently mobile-responsive, developing a dedicated mobile application could further improve participation rates and provide real-time push notifications for evaluation requests and approval statuses.
- **Broadened Institutional Scope**: The system’s infrastructure can be expanded to accommodate academic assessments, faculty evaluations, and other institutional surveys beyond student organization events, providing a centralized analytical hub for the entire campus.
- **Advanced Predictive Dashboards**: Future iterations could incorporate historical trend analysis and predictive modeling to forecast event success based on previous feedback patterns, allowing organizers to proactively address potential issues.
- **Financial Integration**: While the current system handles cash collections and PDF receipts, future versions could integrate with online payment gateways or the university’s existing financial systems to offer more payment options for student organizations.

By implementing these improvements, **EventFlow** will continue to serve as a robust, user-friendly, and intelligent platform that meets the evolving needs of the CSUCC community, fostering an environment of data-driven excellence.
